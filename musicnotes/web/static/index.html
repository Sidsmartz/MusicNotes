How it works

No generative AI is involved, and nothing is invented. The sound is analysed with classic signal processing: onsets, pitches by harmonic summation, chords by template matching with Viterbi smoothing, and drum hits by frequency band. The picture is analysed with a YOLOv8 detector trained on Open Images (it knows guitars, pianos, keyboards, drums, violins and more) and a pose model for your hands. The app then fits the fret-spacing law to the guitar neck, or finds the black-key pattern on the keyboard. The effects are drawn on top of your real video.
